Hey there,

I’ve just finished reviewing the latest iteration of the Nexus Hub Design System (v2.1). The move towards a more rigid 8px grid system is definitely paying off in terms of layout consistency across the dashboard views.

A few specific points from my review:

  • The SideNavBar separation logic feels much cleaner now that we've removed the border-t on items and focused on elevation shifts.
  • We might need to adjust the primary-container contrast ratio for darker themes to ensure AA accessibility for small label text.
  • The new 2xl corner radius on cards really softens the "industrial" feel while maintaining that professional edge we're aiming for.

I've left several detailed comments directly in the Figma file. Let's touch base tomorrow morning at 10 AM to go over the next steps for implementation.
